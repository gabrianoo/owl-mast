## Spring contract consulted

`org.springframework.transaction.reactive.TransactionSynchronization` (Spring Framework 7.0.10, verified against the shipped bytecode) provides `suspend()`, `resume()`, `beforeCommit(boolean)`, `beforeCompletion()`, `afterCommit()` and `afterCompletion(int)`, all returning `Mono<Void>`. `AbstractReactiveTransactionManager.processCommit` runs `triggerBeforeCommit` for the physical transaction and, on error, `doRollbackOnCommitException` before propagating the error. `TransactionSynchronizationManager` exposes `isActualTransactionActive()`, `isSynchronizationActive()`, `registerSynchronization`, and resource bind/unbind on the shared `TransactionContext`, whose `clear()` does not clear resources (as the reviewer observed).
