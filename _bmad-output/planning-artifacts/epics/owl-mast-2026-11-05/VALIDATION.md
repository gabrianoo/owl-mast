## Independent content review and disposition

Two independent reviewers inspected the completed plan and integration contract. Three material findings were corrected:

1. **Atomic audit durability — resolved in OM-01.03.** Successful business mutations commit with their audit record or durable audit-publication intent. Failed/denied attempts use a separate outcome path. A crash after commit but before publication has an explicit recovery acceptance case.
2. **Snapshot rollback of permissions/effects — resolved in OM-18.02, consistent with E16.** Restored installations quarantine ordinary disclosure and external dispatch until current permissions, knowledge eligibility and external-effect status are reconciled. Restore tests cover snapshots predating revocation, withdrawal and sent notifications. Unverifiable cases fail closed.
3. **Native retained-copy limits — resolved in OM-07.02, OM-08.02 and AGENT-INTEGRATION.md.** Revocation applies to managed retrieval/delivery/materialization/refresh. Independent use of retained files/context is outside server enforcement unless a native interception capability is separately qualified.

## Scope and readiness assessment
