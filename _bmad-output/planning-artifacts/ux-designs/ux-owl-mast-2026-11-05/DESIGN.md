## Brand & Style

**Visual contract.** The user delegated visual design and color choices (UX-03 in [.memlog.md](.memlog.md)). The tokens and visual rules below are **delegated design decisions**, not claims about the user's personal preferences. Behavior and unresolved choices live in [EXPERIENCE.md](EXPERIENCE.md). Both spines take precedence over exploratory mocks and imported references.

**Post-review amendments — UX-18–UX-24:** run evidence, skill/memory statistics, audit history, kanban, dependency inspection and connection machine context were added after the completed review round. That round does not validate these additions. Existing tokens, components and observation-only V1 remain the basis. The user-supplied playbook establishes the six kanban stage labels; source mappings remain to be validated.

Owl Mast is a focused workspace for understanding work and receiving useful learning. Neutral ink/slate surfaces make room for readable evidence. Blue marks orientation and navigation; cyan identifies knowledge; amber draws attention to an observed issue. Work status is legible before decorative detail. Modest summaries lead into the records behind them, rather than overwhelming those records.

The user values ConvaDesk's concepts and UX, not its appearance. Ponytail's compact panels, fine boundaries, and selective accents inform the direction: [inspected dark reference](.working/reference-ponytail-board.png), [primary image source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail/main/assets/band/board.png). Neither its branding nor its dark-only presentation is adopted.

The user accepted (UX-13) the displayed colors, mobile appearance, and generated owl mark in the [daily-work study](mockups/daily-work.html); preserve that mark. AISQUAD brand ideas may inform future refinement, but do not replace the accepted mark. The accepted [owl mark](assets/owl-mark.svg) preserves the study's 24×24 geometry, unfilled paths, rounded stroke caps/joins, and 1.8-unit stroke. Use its `currentColor` with the theme's primary token; pair it with the Owl Mast wordmark text. Do not redraw it or substitute another brand asset.

**Delegated design decision:** provide light and dark themes, follow the device theme initially, and allow an explicit user override. Apply every unsuffixed color in light mode and its `-dark` counterpart in dark mode; the mapping applies to component token references too. A component with its own `-dark` entry, such as `action-control-dark`, uses that entry instead of the mapping.

Contrast targets follow [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): normal text **≥4.5:1**, large text **≥3:1**, meaningful control/focus boundaries **≥3:1** against adjacent surfaces. Core text/control pairs and all identity markers pass in both themes in the [exact-palette calculation](assets/palette-contrast.md); primary-fill text measures 6.22:1 light and 8.70:1 dark. This calculation does not validate rendered states, overlays, graphs, or the final app. Standalone references use the [reference theme](assets/reference-theme.css) as an exact-palette fallback. Embedded references can inherit preview-host semantic colors, so those renders do not prove the exact palette. Identity colors retain their measured pairs. Dividers are decorative and must not be the sole control boundary. Do not reduce text opacity or introduce unmeasured accent tints.

**[D] Personal identity palette:** `{colors.identity-iris}`, `{colors.identity-mulberry}`, `{colors.identity-clay}`, and `{colors.identity-slate}` have matching `-dark` tokens. Use these only on a diamond-shaped identity marker, never on status dots, progress, or knowledge labels. Each marker meets ≥3:1 on card and secondary surfaces; the name and fixed type label use main text. Slate is the neutral fallback. Color choices are named; selection includes a text/check indicator. Chart bars use primary fill against secondary surface with labeled data, not identity colors.

## Typography

Use the system sans family; IDs use the system monospace family. No font download is required. Body copy and inputs use `{typography.body}`; compact evidence lists use `{typography.row}`; labels use `{typography.label}`. Key facts, source identity, progress basis, access messages, actions, freshness, field labels, run state, and compact labels remain **at least 14px**. `{typography.meta}` is reserved for supplementary text.

Use `{typography.title}` on wide layouts and `{typography.title-compact}` on compact layouts. Headings use `{typography.heading}`. Counts use tabular numerals; large counts never replace their denominator, period, or measurement label. Allow text scaling and wrapping; truncate only optional previews with an accessible expansion.

## Layout & Spacing

Use the 4/8/12/16/24/32px spacing scale. Wide means 1024px and above; compact means below 1024px. Wide-page margins use `{spacing.page-wide}`; compact margins use `{spacing.page-compact}`. Panels use `{spacing.4}` padding, grouped sections `{spacing.5}` gaps. Controls have a minimum hit area of `{spacing.control-target}` in both dimensions and grow with text.

**Designer proposal:** at 1024px and above, a persistent navigation rail and a flexible two-column main area support summaries, attention, work, and evidence. At 600–1023px, navigation collapses to a labeled menu; compatible panels can share a row. Below 600px, use one reading column and full-width details. Do not shrink information to preserve desktop columns. The main reading area may grow to 1440px; long prose stays near 70 characters per line.

The [daily-work study](mockups/daily-work.html) illustrates local filtering and expandable evidence; [study notes](.working/direction-daily-work-notes.md) explain the intent.

**User-confirmed addition:** Focus is a separate board for assigned work, not the Daily Work landing page. **Designer proposal:** stack readable epic groups, with a combined view available; within each group show observed work and agent contributions. On phone, groups remain vertically stacked and cards retain source/status labels. The [Focus study](mockups/focus.html) demonstrates both arrangements and project filtering; [study notes](.working/key-focus-notes.md) explain shared runs, unmatched work, and the distinction between assigned tickets and whole-epic context.

Each card retains its role and run identity. Models and effort use separate labeled tags, not the agent's identity color. Do not use a location badge as a running or isolation guarantee.

## Elevation & Depth

Use tonal surfaces and fine dividers for hierarchy. Persistent panels have no shadow. A temporary overlaid detail may use a restrained shadow only to distinguish it from the retained page; that shadow must not replace its boundary or focus handling. Ordinary evidence expansion stays in the reading flow. Glow, animated pulse, and decorative blur are optional, unselected effects; these references do not establish a need for them.

## Shapes

Panels use `{rounded.lg}` (12px), controls and nested evidence `{rounded.md}`, and progress-evidence markers `{rounded.sm}`. `{rounded.full}` is reserved for chips and small identity/status markers. Rounded progress tracks remain supplemental to text. Unknown progress has no filled bar; it uses a readable text state.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep source, scope, denominator, and freshness readable beside the claim. | Use a big percentage to imply accepted delivery. |
| Use color with text/icon/state cues and measured contrast. | Make color the only evidence of status or reduce supporting text to illegibility. |
| Preserve focused detail and meaningful reading order across devices. | Force a dense dependency graph or wide table into phone-sized text. |
| Give unknown, partial, conflicting, and unavailable information their own visible states. | Fill an absent metric with zero or a guessed progress bar. |
| Treat the mockups as exploratory and the exact tokens as this draft's visual contract. | Infer a framework, storage system, accepted navigation, or successful connector from the mock. |
