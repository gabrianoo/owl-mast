The requested audit scope covers every record Owl Mast creates, including later changes and outcomes. Knowledge and skills may be an initial implementation slice only if sequencing requires it; that does not reduce the target.

Audit integrity and access

Correct through a later event. A correction references the earlier event and explains what changed. Earlier history is not silently rewritten in the user view; retention, storage and tamper-evidence guarantees remain architecture decisions.

Successful records and failed attempts differ. An attempted publication or delivery is not a successful creation. Delayed or unsupported audit collection shows coverage gaps, not “No activity”.

History keeps permission boundaries. Titles, actors, diffs, reasons, recipient details and source links require their own current authorization. A reader with no access receives a neutral unavailable state, without a hidden-record count.

No secrets or automatic raw-content retention. Exact versions and permitted changes can be inspected without storing credentials, prompts or the complete source body in the audit stream. Content-retention defaults remain open under A-3.
