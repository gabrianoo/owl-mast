How stages relate to evidence +

In this fixture every stage assignment has a recorded source basis. Jira “In Progress” alone cannot distinguish Design, Build or Test. Concurrent build/test contributions remain inspectable without duplicating the ticket subtotal.

Accepted artifacts and observed handoffs may explain stage movement, but Owl Mast does not trigger the next activity. Stale mapping and conflicting sources retain an explicit warning rather than silently moving cards.
